Mimir

A spirit who keeps falling onward.

Mimir is the character the product is made from. In the story he falls from world to world, and he attaches himself to an ordinary person so their days become a chapter. In the shop, that person is whoever is holding him.

What you do with him

Hold an unlocked phone against him once. He is paired. In the evening you leave a mood, a note, a photograph, and the app writes it into an adventure log in a warm voice. The spirit himself has no battery. The story lives with the gem he carries.

What happens when you pass him on

Giving him away is the point. Your chapter freezes. The next person pairs him and continues. You can follow where he goes. The gem is the thread through every owner.

The app’s base — your own writing, the handover, and a look at the new chapter — is free with every spirit. Premium is a one-time unlock. There is no subscription.
